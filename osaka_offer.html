The Problem

You need a go/no-go before you spend real money

The two most common deal-killers in Osaka minpaku — condominium management prohibition and ward-level operating restrictions — are not visible on property listings and are not checked by most buying agents until after you've engaged them and paid a deposit. A third of shortlisted properties fail on one of these two points.

The Standard Minpaku path (180-day cap) looks attractive on a price sheet. The unit economics only hold in specific wards, for specific building types, above specific ADR thresholds. Getting those numbers wrong by 15% on occupancy or 20% on management fees produces a negative-cash-flow asset dressed as a yield play.

This screen gives you a structured answer — scored, documented, and ready to show a partner or banker — before you move to LOI.
